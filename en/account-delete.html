Delete your Ssokvoca account

Ssokvoca (쏙보카, "the App") · Operator: soarus (Representative: Seongyeop Kim)

Linking an account is optional. You can delete a linked account in either of the ways below. For how we handle data, see the Privacy Policy.

1. Delete in the App

Open Settings → Account → Delete account, confirm again with the sign-in method you used (Apple or Google), then tap Delete.

2. If you already removed the App or can't use it

Email soaruskr@gmail.com with the subject "Account deletion request" (계정 삭제 요청). In the message, include:

  • The sign-in method (Apple or Google)
  • The email address you linked (if you used Apple's "Hide My Email", that relay address or your Apple ID email)

After verifying it is you, we will delete the account within 7 days and reply by email.
