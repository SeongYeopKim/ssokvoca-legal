Ssokvoca (쏙보카, "the App") · Operator: soarus (Representative: Seongyeop Kim) · Effective: the App's release date

The App has no sign-up or login and does not send your study data to any server operated by us.

1. Information and purposes

TypeDataPurpose · where it is kept
Study recordsKanji studied, review schedule, quiz results, settings (exam date, reminder time)To provide learning features. Stored only on your device; we cannot see it.
PurchasesWhether you hold a subscription or lifetime accessTo unlock paid content. Payments are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never receive card or payment details. The App only asks the store on your device whether you own a product.
Error reports & inquiriesYour email address and message (the App pre-fills the kanji/word ID, app version and device type)To fix content errors and reply. Received only when you choose to send an email.

2. Retention and deletion

Study records on your device are deleted when you delete the App. Inquiry emails are deleted one year after they are resolved.

3. Sharing

We do not sell, share or outsource processing of personal information. Payments are between you and Apple/Google under their privacy policies. Inquiry emails are received through Google (Gmail).

4. Ads, analytics and tracking

The App contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs and no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

5. Notifications, widget and speech

Study reminders and the home-screen widget are generated on your device. Japanese pronunciation uses your device's text-to-speech engine, which may be handled by your operating system provider depending on your settings.

6. Your rights

You may request access to, correction or deletion of any personal information we hold about you (inquiry emails) by contacting us below. Residents of the EU/UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA) have these rights by law; we honor them for everyone.

7. Children

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or 16 in the EU).
