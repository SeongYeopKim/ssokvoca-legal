Privacy Policy

Ssokvoca (쏙보카, "the App") · Operator: soarus (Representative: Seongyeop Kim) · Effective: November 22, 2026 (applies from app version 1.0.2)

We comply with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea and other applicable laws, and handle only the minimum information needed to run the App. All features work without signing in. If you link an Apple or Google account, your study records are stored in the operator's cloud (Google Firebase) so you can continue on another device.

2. Retention and deletion

Study records on your device are deleted when you delete the App. Account information and your study records in the cloud are deleted without delay when you delete your account (only an empty marker showing the account was deleted remains; it contains no personal information). Usage statistics are kept for up to 14 months from collection and error reports for up to 90 days, then deleted automatically. Error-report and inquiry emails are deleted without delay one year after the day we finish handling them. Electronic files are deleted in a way that cannot be recovered.

3. Outsourcing and transfer abroad

We do not provide personal information to third parties. To run the App, we outsource processing as shown below, and this involves transferring information abroad.

Payments take place between you and Apple or Google and are governed by their privacy policies. Inquiry emails are received through Google (Gmail).

4. Ads and tracking

The App has no ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites. It does not read the advertising identifier (IDFA or Advertising ID).

5. Notifications, widget and speech

Study reminders and the home-screen widget are generated on your device and are not sent anywhere. Japanese pronunciation uses your device's text-to-speech engine (depending on your device settings, it may be handled by your operating system provider).

6. Your rights and account deletion

You may request access to, correction, deletion or suspension of processing of the personal information we hold about you (account information, cloud study records, inquiry emails). Contact us below and we will handle it without delay. Residents of the EU/UK (GDPR) and California (CCPA/CPRA) have these rights by law; we honor them for everyone.

Account deletion: you can delete your account right away in the App under Settings → Account → Delete account. If you have already removed the App, request deletion by email as explained in How to delete your account. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; please cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

You can delete study records on your device yourself through the App's settings or by deleting the App.

7. Children under 14

Only users aged 14 or older may link an account, and we ask you to confirm you are 14 or older when you link. Using the App without an account has no age limit. If we learn that information of a child under 14 was collected through an account, we will delete it without delay. A child under 14 who sends us an inquiry email should have the consent of a legal guardian.

8. Security measures

Study records in the cloud are protected by access rules so that only the account holder can read or write them, and data is encrypted in transit. The admin console and inquiry email are accessed only from accounts with two-step verification enabled, and are not used for any purpose beyond handling.
